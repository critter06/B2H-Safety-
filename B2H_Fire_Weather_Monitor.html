How this works: Pulls current relative humidity and wind speed from the nearest NWS station to each corridor segment (api.weather.gov, updates ~hourly), and separately checks api.weather.gov/alerts/active for any official Red Flag Warning or Fire Weather Watch covering that segment's coordinates. A segment shows SHUTDOWN when RH is below 25% AND sustained wind exceeds 6 mph — both conditions must be true. The Red Flag indicator is separate and independent — it reflects the National Weather Service's own official warning product, which uses different criteria and can be active even when the project's RH/wind trigger is not, or vice versa. Treat a Red Flag Warning as a serious independent signal regardless of the project trigger's status.
Important: Station readings represent the nearest ASOS/airport, which may differ from on-site conditions in canyons, ridgetops, or river/valley segments. Station data does not replace on-site measurement — crews must verify with a calibrated handheld at the work location and log readings. When in doubt, stop. · Stations: KHRI, KPDT, KMEH, KLGD, KBKE, KONO.
